Google Earth Engine Training

Master cloud-based satellite image analysis from beginner to professional level.

Google Earth Engine (GEE) is a free online platform developed by Google that allows you to analyse millions of satellite images without downloading them. Instead of using your computer to process huge files, Google's cloud computers do the work for you.

Simple Example
Imagine you want to compare how Ilorin looked in 2000 and 2025. Instead of downloading hundreds of Landsat images, GEE lets you display both years within a few seconds.
Practice Exercise
Open Google Earth Engine, search for Ilorin, Nigeria, and zoom into the city.

The Earth Engine interface has four important parts: the Code Editor, the Map, the Console, and the Inspector. The Code Editor is where you write commands, while the Map displays your results.

Simple Example
The Inspector works like clicking Google Maps. When you click on any location, it shows the coordinates and information about that point.
Practice Exercise
Click on the Asa River near Ilorin using the Inspector and observe the coordinates that appear.

Google Earth Engine uses JavaScript to control maps and satellite images. The first thing you'll learn is how to create variables and display results.

var city = "Ilorin"; print(city);
Simple Example
A variable is like writing someone's name on a file. Instead of writing "Ilorin" repeatedly, you simply use the variable called city.
Practice Exercise
Change "Ilorin" to "Abuja" and run the code again.

Satellite images are stored inside Image Collections. An Image Collection contains thousands of satellite images taken over different dates.

Simple Example
Instead of opening one Landsat image after another, you can ask Earth Engine to show every Landsat image captured over Abuja during 2024.
Practice Exercise
Filter Landsat images between January and December 2024.

Satellite images often contain clouds that hide the land below. Earth Engine allows us to choose the clearest image before analysis.

landsat.sort("CLOUD_COVER").first();
Simple Example
Imagine taking ten photographs during rainy weather. You naturally choose the clearest one. Earth Engine does exactly the same thing.
Practice Exercise
Select the clearest Landsat image over Ilorin.

Sentinel-2 provides higher-resolution images than Landsat, making buildings, roads and farms much easier to identify.

Simple Example
A newly constructed road in Lagos appears much clearer in Sentinel-2 than in Landsat.
Practice Exercise
Display Sentinel-2 imagery over Lagos and compare it with Landsat.

NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) measures how healthy vegetation is. Healthy plants appear greener because they reflect more Near Infrared light.

Simple Example
A healthy maize farm appears bright green, while dry grass appears yellow or brown.
Practice Exercise
Calculate NDVI for farmland around Ilorin and identify the healthiest vegetation.

NDWI (Normalized Difference Water Index) helps identify rivers, lakes and wetlands.

Simple Example
After heavy rainfall, NDWI highlights flooded areas much more clearly than an ordinary satellite image.
Practice Exercise
Highlight the Asa River near Ilorin using NDWI.

Land Use Classification groups land into categories such as Built-up Areas, Vegetation, Water, Farmland and Bare Land.

Simple Example
If Ilorin expands into nearby farmland, classification makes those changes easy to identify.
Practice Exercise
Create a simple five-class land-cover map.

After completing your analysis, you'll export your maps for reports, research and professional GIS projects.

Simple Example
Export your NDVI map as a PNG for a university assignment or as a GeoTIFF for further GIS analysis.
Final Practical Project
Create an NDVI map of Ilorin, add a title and legend, and export the final map.
